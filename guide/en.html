How to play

Everyone knows the same secret word except one person: the imposter. Take turns giving clues and find the imposter. If you catch them, they still get one chance to guess the word.

Quick start

  1. Create a room. On the main menu tap Create online room. Set a 6-digit room PIN, optionally name the room (e.g. "Friday night") and pick the rules.
  2. Join as a player. Once the room exists, enter your name and a 4-digit personal PIN, like everyone else.
  3. Invite friends. Tap the room code at the top or Invite link and send the link.
  4. Start the round. With at least 3 players, tap Start round. Everyone looks at their own card and keeps it hidden.
  5. Give clues and vote. Follow the speaking order, give one-word clues, then vote for the imposter.

Online room

Everyone plays and votes on their own phone. No account is needed.

  • Joining: Type the room code on the main menu and tap Go to room, or open the invite link. Choose I'm new and enter your name and a 4-digit PIN.
  • Open vs. private: Open rooms show up in the Open rooms list on the main menu. Private rooms can only be joined with the code. The host can switch this in Edit rules.
  • Tabs: Game shows the current step, Players shows who is in the room (green dot), Scores shows the score table and round history.
  • Going back to the main menu does not mean leaving. Your seat and points stay.

Single-phone room

One phone is passed around the table. You type the player names when creating the room.

  1. The person whose name is on screen takes the phone, taps show my card, looks, then taps Hide and pass it on.
  2. When everyone has seen their card, the speaking order appears. Give your clues.
  3. Vote out loud. Pick the person with the most votes on the phone and confirm. If it is a tie, tap The vote was a tie.

How a round works

  1. Card: Every card shows the category and the word. The imposter's card says You are the imposter instead of the word. Depending on the rules, the imposter may or may not see the category.
  2. Clues: In speaking order, each player gives one clue about the word. Never say the word itself. The imposter acts like they know it.
  3. Vote: Tap the person you suspect, then Confirm my vote. You can change your pick before confirming.
  4. Tie: If the vote is tied, everyone gives one more clue and you vote again.
  5. Caught: If the chosen player is the imposter, they get 1 minute to guess the word out loud.
  6. Decision: When time is up, the other players vote Got it / Missed it. The majority decides.

Scores and round history are saved in the room. Come back the next day and continue where you left off.

What the PINs are for

  • Personal PIN (4 digits): If you come back to the room from another phone, choose I'm returning, pick your name and enter your PIN. You get your seat and points back.
  • Room PIN (6 digits): The host can take the room back from another device by choosing I'm the host and entering this PIN with the room code.
  • Nobody can see the PINs, not even the host. Keep them to yourself and avoid easy ones like 123456.

What the host can do

  • Start or cancel rounds and change the rules (word language, categories, number of imposters, whether the imposter sees the category, open/private).
  • Remove a player. Their name then shows removed by the host, and their points are kept.
  • Approve or reject name change requests.
  • Rename the room in Edit rules.
  • If a player disconnects, the game does not stop. The host is asked to Continue without them or Cancel round.
  • Before leaving, hand the room to another player with Make host. The new host sets their own room PIN.
  • Delete the room for good with Delete room at the bottom of the Players tab (tap twice). Scores, history and PINs are deleted too and cannot be restored.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. The app gives each device an anonymous identity in the background. It never asks for your email or phone number.

Can the same word come up again?

In a room, a word is not repeated until every word in the selected categories has been played. Each language keeps its own list.

Which languages are supported?

The interface is available in Turkish, Arabic and English. The word language is chosen per room, and each language has its own word list. Everyone can switch the interface language on their own phone.

I changed phones or cleared my browser data.

Open the room, choose I'm returning, pick your name and enter your personal PIN.

What data is stored?

Only what the game needs: room code, player names, game state, votes and scores. Rooms that stay unused for a long time may be deleted.
